# Hoot Unfathomably Privacy Policy

Hoot Unfathomably is a client for an Unfathomably, Pleroma, Rebased, or
compatible server selected by you. The app communicates directly with that
server to sign in, retrieve timelines and notifications, and perform actions
you request, such as publishing, replying, boosting, or reacting.

The app does not operate its own analytics or telemetry service and does not
send your account data to a Hoot-operated server. Your selected server and any
media hosts it uses may collect information under their own policies, including
your IP address and activity on that server.

Your access token is kept in the operating system's secure credential storage.
Non-sensitive account metadata and app preferences are stored locally so the
app can restore your selected account. Logging out removes the locally stored
access token for that account. You should review the privacy policy of every
server you choose to use.

GPS path recording uses foreground location only after you press Start
recording. The app does not request background or always-on location access.
An unfinished route and its precise points are stored in the app's local data
so the draft can be recovered. The points are sent to your selected server only
after you review the route and confirm publication. Exporting GPX or explicitly
opening the route start in OpenStreetMap shares location data with the Android
destination you choose. Discarding the draft removes the locally stored copy.

Reading-shelf changes are sent to your selected server through its book-library
API. They do not create timeline posts, but their visibility and federation are
controlled by that server. Reviews, comments, and quotations are federated posts
and use the visibility you select before publishing.

The app keeps bounded, account-scoped copies of recently loaded timelines,
profiles, saved posts, lists, groups, and notifications so they remain readable
during a network interruption. Composer drafts, copied draft media, media
descriptions, accessibility preferences, notification preferences, and saved
non-secret account metadata are also stored on the device. Drafts and caches are
not sent anywhere until you explicitly publish or perform another server action.

Scheduled posts are stored by your selected server after you press Schedule.
Deleting a local draft does not cancel an already scheduled server post; use the
Scheduled posts screen for that action. Cross-account actions contact each
selected account's own server only after you confirm the action.

Post translation is requested from your selected server. That server may use a
translation provider under its own privacy policy. Native reports are sent only
after you press Send report and are received by your server's moderators; the
optional forwarding control may also send the report to the remote account's
server.

Android notification preferences and seen identifiers are stored per account
on the device. Local notification text is derived from notification data already
returned by your selected server. Quiet hours defer eligible alerts until a
later background check rather than sending them to another service.
